BIOGLOW · 2026–27

Learning to move an arm.

Our Pybricks experiment: find zero, move, pause, return.

  1. Find the mechanical stop with motor duty limited to 25%.
  2. Reset the angle to 0°; this labels the position without moving.
  3. Move to 180°, 90°, then 120°. Pause two seconds at each.
  4. Return to 0° and hold.

Targets are measured from zero, not added to the previous angle.

Peek at our Python code

Uses Motor, Port, Stop, and wait.

right_arm = Motor(Port.F, gears=[[28, 36], [12, 20]])

right_arm.run_until_stalled(-300, then=Stop.HOLD, duty_limit=25)
right_arm.reset_angle(0)
right_arm.run_target(100, 180, then=Stop.HOLD)
wait(2000)
right_arm.run_target(100, 90, then=Stop.HOLD)
wait(2000)
right_arm.run_target(100, 120, then=Stop.HOLD)
wait(2000)
right_arm.run_target(100, 0, then=Stop.HOLD)

Port F: arm motor. Gear lists: two gear stages. Stop.HOLD: hold the target.
