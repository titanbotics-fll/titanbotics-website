What is FLL?

What is
FIRST LEGO League?

Big ideas. Small robots. Amazing teamwork.

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is a global, hands-on STEM program where kids explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics using LEGO technology. Teams ask questions, try ideas, and learn to solve problems together.

What do FLL teams do?

In FIRST LEGO League Challenge, teams bring these three things together.

Build & Code

Design, build, and program a LEGO robot to complete missions on a playing field. Test an idea, learn what happens, and try again.

Innovate

Explore a real-world problem through an Innovation Project. Ask questions, imagine a solution, and share your ideas and what you learn.

Work Together

Listen, share the work, and make room for every idea. Practice creativity, communication, and kindness—even during competition.

More than just robots

FIRST Core Values

Success goes beyond points on the field. Teams develop their robot design and innovation ideas while putting these six values into practice.

Discovery
Stay curious and learn something new.
Innovation
Try creative ideas and keep improving.
Impact
Use what you learn to help others.
Inclusion
Welcome everyone and value their ideas.
Teamwork
Share the challenge and grow together.
Fun
Enjoy the adventure and celebrate each other.
